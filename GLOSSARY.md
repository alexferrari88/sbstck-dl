# Substack Archive

## Language

**Post**:
A publication's article, including its body, publication date, title, and associated media.

**Archive**:
A local collection of downloaded posts in one output format, with an index and retained metadata for later downloads.

**Archive entry**:
A downloaded post's membership in an archive, including its local file, descriptive metadata, download time, and optional local cover image.

**Cover image**:
The image representing a post in an archive index. It can share a downloaded body image when both refer to the same source.

**Archive workflow**:
One download run's restoration and update of the selected archives, including cover reconciliation and publication of their metadata and indexes.

**Exported book**:
A single ebook containing selected archived posts as chapters, with a table of contents and embedded reading images. It is derived from a local archive and is separate from that archive's posts, metadata, and index.
